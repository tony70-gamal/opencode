## Notes per platform
- **Instagram**: carousels up to 10 slides, 1080x1350 renders largest in feed. Keep the first
  slide readable as a thumbnail — it is the whole click decision. Put the link in the caption
  AND in a "link in bio" story frame, because IG captions are not clickable for everyone.
- **Facebook**: 1200x630 (`images/facebook/`) is the largest the feed renders. Paste the link
  in the caption body so the preview card picks it up. `images/facebook/fb_link_business.jpg`
  is the business-model variant.
- **TikTok**: 1080x1920 only, 18-35s is the sweet spot here. Add music in-app at low volume
  under the voiceover script in `captions/REELS_TIKTOK.md`. First frame is the cover still, so
  the hook is visible before the video plays.
- **LinkedIn**: 1280x720 or 1080x1080 for link posts; 1920x1080 for native video. 4:5 portrait
  is accepted but renders smaller in the feed — reserve it for carousel-style posts.
- **YouTube**: `videos/landscape_hero_16x9.mp4` plus `images/youtube/thumb_problem.png` as the
  thumbnail.
