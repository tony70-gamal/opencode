## Post 1 — Link post with `fb_link_cover.jpg`

**Headline (paste into the link preview title):**
Smart Biogas: turning 18M tons of Egyptian food waste into measurable energy

**Caption**
Egypt throws away about 18 million tons of food waste every year, and 80–88% of it ends up in
open dumps. At the same time, 81–82% of our electricity runs on imported natural gas.

So we pay to throw waste away, then pay again to import the energy that same waste could have
produced. That is the problem we took on.

Our answer is a closed loop: organic waste is shredded, fed into a sealed 18 litre digester
held at 35°C, converted to biogas, used for heat or electricity, and monitored in real time by
2× ESP32 controllers logging temperature, pH, pressure, methane and hydrogen sulfide over
MQTT/TLS into a TimescaleDB historian with a live Grafana and AI layer.

This is not a slide deck. We have built an 18 litre mobile rig — 100×50×70 cm on casters — with
a real gas line, real sensors and seven independent safety layers. The mechanical relief valve
never depends on software.

The next step is a 60 litre pilot running 30 to 90 days, so the business case is built on
measured yield instead of assumptions.

If your organisation generates organic waste every day and wants to see what it is worth,
open the project here: smart-biogas2026.surge.sh

We are looking for pilot partners: universities first, then restaurants and hotels, food
processing, and agricultural operations.
